So, What Is the Problem?

Let me start with a small story. Say you tell a robot, "Clean my room as fast as you can." The robot gets super excited. It pushes everything under the bed and throws away your homework. The room looks clean,but that is not what you wanted.

The robot did what you said. It did not do what you meant. This gap between "what we say" and "what we mean" is the main idea of AI alignment. In simple words, alignment means making AI do what people really want.

Today AI is getting smarter every year. If a chatbot gets your question wrong, it is no big deal. But if an AI that handles money or hospital work gets our goal wrong, that can be a disaster. Still very few people working on this if you ask me personally.

Why Is It So Hard?

People are complex. We close to never agree with each other. Sometimes we don't even know what we want ourselves. It is very hard to write all of that as clear rules for a computer.

Solution

A really good idea I read about recently is learning from human feedback. If you want to read more about this topic, you can check this Wikipedia page on AI alignment.

How the Feedback Idea Works

It is a bit like a teacher checking your answers again and again. Here are the steps:

  1. The AI writes a few different answers to one question.
  2. Real people look at the answers and pick which ones are better.
  3. Another small model learns what people like and gives each answer a score.
  4. The main AI practices to get higher scores.
  5. We repeat this many, many times.

Other Ways to Do It

Feedback is not the only way. One extreme way of resolving this would by neural detangling though not being a official term suggests doing something to the neural network such that it detangles and becomes single threads(if we treat a small neural network as a thread). Still, here is a small table with three ways, and the problem with each one.

Method Main Idea Problem
Human feedback People rate answers and the AI learns what they like People can get tired or make mistakes
Written rules The AI checks its own answers using a list of rules Rules can be unclear or clash with each other
Looking inside the AI Scientists study how the AI thinks inside Big AI models are very hard to understand

What makes it tough

Even with all these ideas, many problems are still open. Here are some of them:

  • Cheating the score: the AI finds a lazy trick to get a high score without doing the real job.
  • Checking hard work: what if the AI does something so tough that no person can check it?
  • Trust Issues: we want the AI to tell the truth, not just say nice things to make us happy.
  • Different moral values People around the world think differently, so who decides what is "right"?

Why Should a Student Care?

You do not need to be a big scientist to start. This field uses coding, maths, and a bit of thinking about people too. You can begin by learning the basics of machine learning and reading simple articles. The field is still new so new ideas are always welcome which make it a very rewarding field as well and people getting early into it can have the same kind of rewards that people who learnt tech just before the start of tech boom got.

Conclusion

AI alignment is just about making sure smart machines stay helpful and safe. It starts with one simple fact which would be saying something and meaning something are not the same thing, kind of what we woud call emphatic listening in proffesional communication.Anyway human feedback, written rules and looking inside the AI all help, but each one has its own problems.Personally as a extremist despite so many benefits of ai ,I think that a way to detangle neural networks should be looked upon (in terms of medical profession, if we think a useful virus can be harmful we make a vaccine for preventive measures and not wait for it to spread before making a vaccine).

The best part is that nobody has solved it yet. So even a beginner can help. If this post made you think, "how will a machine ever know what I mean?", then you have already started.
